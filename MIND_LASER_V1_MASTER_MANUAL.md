### Technical Specifications & Frequencies
The system monitors five distinct electroencephalographic wave-bands:
*   **Delta ($\Delta$) [0.5–4.0 Hz]**: Biomarker for deep metabolic recovery and cellular restoration. High levels imply mental depletion, high fatigue, or physical rest.
*   **Theta ($\theta$) [4.0–8.0 Hz]**: The signature of flow state, deep meditation, inward-focused visualization, and high-level subconscious problem-solving.
*   **Alpha ($\alpha$) [8.0–12.0 Hz]**: Reflects relaxed alertness, high cognitive cushion, mental clarity, and readiness for fast logical tasks.
*   **Beta ($\beta$) [12.0–30.0 Hz]**: Indicates active analytical logic, spatial reasoning, intense concentration, and processing of external sensory inputs. Over-concentration spikes Beta, leading to stress.
*   **Gamma ($\gamma$) [30.0–100.0 Hz]**: The rarest and fastest band, indicating creative synthesis, lateral breakthrough, cohesive mental processing, and flash insights.
